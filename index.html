01 / The big picture

A plan with room to wander.

Two shared Disney days cover all four Walt Disney World parks. November 15 is for the details you want to explore solo. November 23 is a relaxed fireworks night outside the parks.

Important fine print: At participating WDW table-service restaurants, gratuity of 18% is calculated on the pre-discount check. Present the certificate before ordering, bring a valid company ID, and confirm how many guests your specific certificate covers. Reservations and options

Liberty Tree Tavern is the Thanksgiving-style family meal. Keeping 7:50 PM lets you enjoy EPCOT rather than rushing back for an early dinner.

Hollywood Brown Derby has the most compelling upscale dinner potential with the 50% certificate, if you can reserve it for Nov 24.

Backup: Hollywood & Vine for a festive buffet with characters, also on the eligible restaurant list.

Note: Dining packages such as Fantasmic! dining packages are excluded from these certificates, even if the regular restaurant participates.

05 / Getting around

Build in the travel time.

Between Disney parks

  • 01
    Animal Kingdom → Hollywood Studios
    Direct park-to-park Disney bus. Allow about 45–75 minutes door to door.
  • 02
    Magic Kingdom ↔ EPCOT
    Monorail and a transfer at the Transportation and Ticket Center. Budget about 60 minutes.
  • 03
    Driving
    Consider moving the car to the park where you finish so you don't have to retrieve it after Animal Kingdom closes.

While you're there

My Disney Experience is the main app for admission, dining, wait times, entertainment schedules and directions.

For Nov 23 fireworks, confirm how you will reach the Polynesian. Resort parking and beach access can be restricted; the Transportation and Ticket Center is an alternate transportation starting point.

Florida parks are much more spread out than Disneyland Resort. Do not plan a park hop like the five-minute walk across the Anaheim Esplanade.
